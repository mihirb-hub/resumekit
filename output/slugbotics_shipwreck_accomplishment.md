# Slugbotics: Python & CAD Accomplishment

During my time with Slugbotics, I led the development of critical vision systems for our competition-entering Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV). I utilized extensive CAD modeling in SolidWorks to design and prototype robust, waterproof camera enclosures capable of withstanding harsh aquatic environments while securely mounting the hardware to the vehicle's frame. Beyond the mechanical integration, I leveraged Python to process and analyze a massive dataset of benthic imagery, engineering computer vision pipelines to autonomously analyze and detect various shipwreck types. By bridging complex mechanical design with advanced software analytics, I delivered a comprehensive mechatronic vision suite that significantly enhanced our ROV's target identification capabilities during the international competition.
