# SCIPP: Most Impressive Accomplishment

The most impressive thing I have accomplished was validating a complex prototype system designed for the creation of medical isotopes during my time at the Santa Cruz Institute for Particle Physics (SCIPP). Because the underlying hardware required extreme precision, I designed and executed rigorous testing protocols utilizing high-frequency oscilloscopes to monitor subtle electrical anomalies and specialized light filters to precisely isolate optical sensor signals. Furthermore, I subjected the entire custom electro-optical assembly to rigorous environmental stress testing inside thermal chambers, ensuring the system maintained absolute reliability under harsh conditions. By methodically debugging hardware failures across these diverse test environments, I helped accelerate the development of a critical piece of technology with the potential to significantly advance modern medical imaging and treatments.
